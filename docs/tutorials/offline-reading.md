# Read offline

Status: Approved by the user and integrated into the in-app catalog, 2026-10-09. Part of the [tutorials and authoring guide](../tutorials.md).

Topic ID: `offline_reading`.

Summary: Bring a good book along, even when the connection stays behind.

## 1. Pack a book for the journey

Step ID: `prepare_book`

Train ride, quiet getaway, or just a patchy connection? Save your book, then open it online and let the chapters load. Bookshelf keeps the loaded content of saved books for offline reading. Saving alone does not download the text.

## 2. Try a little offline reading

Step ID: `check_offline`

Before you head out, check Contents for unavailable chapters and try reopening the book without an internet connection. If the pages you want are there, you are ready to settle in. Only stored chapters will be readable offline.

## 3. Refill your offline shelf

Step ID: `restore_downloads`

Clearing the Offline reading cache in Settings > Storage & Offline removes stored book content. Your saved-book list and reading position remain.

Open your saved books online again before relying on them offline.
