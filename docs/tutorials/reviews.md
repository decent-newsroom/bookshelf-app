# Share your take on a book

Status: Approved by the user and integrated into the in-app catalog, 2026-10-09. Part of the [tutorials and authoring guide](../tutorials.md).

Topic ID: `reviews`.

Summary: Give a book some stars and help another reader find their next good read.

## 1. Your turn to weigh in

Step ID: `open_review`

Loved it, puzzled by it, still thinking about it? Press and hold the book card and choose Rate and review. Connect your account first if you want to publish your thoughts.

## 2. A few stars and your own words

Step ID: `publish_review`

Choose one to five stars. Your opinion is optional: a sentence about what stayed with you can be enough. Tap Publish review and review the request in your signer. Your rating and any written review are public on Nostr.

If delivery has to wait for a connection, Bookshelf keeps the signed review for retry.

## 3. Give it another thought

Step ID: `edit_review`

A reread can change your mind. Open Rate and review again with the same account to load your existing review. Adjust the stars or your words, then tap Publish changes and review the signer request.
