# Start reading

Status: Approved by the user and integrated into the in-app catalog, 2026-10-09. Part of the [tutorials and authoring guide](../tutorials.md).

Topic ID: `getting_started`.

Summary: Pick a book, make the page feel right, and settle into a good read.

## 1. A good read starts here

Step ID: `open_and_read`

Something catch your eye? On Home, browse the shelves and tap a book to open it. Scroll at your own pace and enjoy the first few pages. You can start without an account.

For your first visit, stay connected while the chapters load. Entries marked as library cards have book details but no readable text.

## 2. Find your way around

Step ID: `reader_controls`

Ready for another chapter? Tap the reading area to show the menus, choose Contents, and tap an available chapter to jump in. Use Back to return to Home when you feel like browsing again.

## 3. Make the page feel right

Step ID: `reading_comfort`

Bigger letters? A little more breathing room? Show the reader menus and tap Aa. Try Font size, Line height, and Text alignment until reading feels comfortable.

Choose Paper, Sepia, or Night to set the mood. The theme carries through the rest of the app too. For a different font, visit Settings > Reading & Display. Bookshelf remembers your choices.
