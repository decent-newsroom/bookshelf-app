# Search

Status: Approved by the user and integrated into the in-app catalog, 2026-10-09. Part of the [tutorials and authoring guide](../tutorials.md).

Topic ID: `search`.

Summary: Find your next good read—or that passage you want to revisit.

## 1. Follow your curiosity

Step ID: `search_books`

Have an author, title, or favourite subject in mind? On Home, tap Search. Leave Search book contents off to look through book details, enter your search, and tap Search. Tap a book that catches your eye to open it.

## 2. Find the words inside

Step ID: `search_chapters`

Remember a phrase, or want to see what books say about a topic? Turn on Search book contents to search chapter text instead of book details. Enter 4 to 160 characters and tap Search. Searching chapters can take a little longer.

## 3. Jump into a match

Step ID: `open_match`

Read the chapter title and excerpt for a taste of each result. Found something interesting? Tap Open matching chapter when it is available to go straight to that chapter.

To look for titles, authors, or subjects again, turn Search book contents off.
