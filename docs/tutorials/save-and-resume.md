# Save a book and return to it

Status: Approved by the user and integrated into the in-app catalog, 2026-10-09. Part of the [tutorials and authoring guide](../tutorials.md).

Topic ID: `save_and_resume`.

Summary: Keep a good find close and pick up where you left off.

## 1. Make room on your shelf

Step ID: `save_book`

Found a keeper? With the book open, show the reader menus and tap Save. You can also press and hold a book card and choose Add to My Books. Your saved books are waiting in My Books.

Saving works without an account. When connected to an account, saving can ask your signer to share your bookshelf publicly.

## 2. Leave a little for later

Step ID: `resume_reading`

Read a little or a lot, then use Back when it is time for a break. Bookshelf remembers your reading position on this device.

On Home, Continue reading opens your most recently opened saved book. You can also reopen a book from My Books. You do not need to turn on tracking to resume reading.
