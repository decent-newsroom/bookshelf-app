# Connect your account

Status: Approved by the user and integrated into the in-app catalog, 2026-10-09. Part of the [tutorials and authoring guide](../tutorials.md).

Topic ID: `connect_account`.

Summary: Bring your Nostr identity along to sync your shelf and share your thoughts.

## 1. Bring your reading voice along

Step ID: `connect_signer`

Ready to share a favourite passage or a review? Connect your Nostr identity with a compatible Android signer. Open Settings > Account & Sync, tap Connect account, and review the request in your signer.

Browsing, reading, and saving books work without an account, so you can connect whenever you feel ready.

## 2. Choose what you share

Step ID: `review_sharing`

Connecting enables bookshelf sync and signed reviews. Saving books while connected can ask your signer to share your bookshelf publicly. Review those requests before approving them.

Reading lists and finished books have separate sharing choices in Settings > Reading progress & privacy. Check those choices before sharing your reading activity.
