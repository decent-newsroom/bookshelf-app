# Keep the lines you love

Status: Approved by the user and integrated into the in-app catalog, 2026-10-09. Part of the [tutorials and authoring guide](../tutorials.md).

Topic ID: `highlights`.

Summary: Save a passage that makes you smile, think, or want to read it twice.

## 1. Catch a favourite line

Step ID: `create_highlight`

Some sentences deserve a second visit. Long-press a passage and drag to select the words you want, then tap Create highlight. It is saved privately on this device; no account needed.

If the same words appear more than once in the chapter, include a little more surrounding text so Bookshelf can identify the passage.

## 2. Revisit your favourites

Step ID: `revisit_highlights`

Tap the reading area to show the menus, then choose Highlights. Browse your saved passages and tap Open passage to return to their chapter.

Changed your mind about a private highlight? Use Delete while it is still private and has not been queued for publication.

## 3. Pass a good line along

Step ID: `share_highlight`

Want to share what caught your eye? With an account connected, choose Publish on a private highlight. Add an optional comment, tap Publish highlight, and review the request in your signer. Publishing shares the passage and comment publicly on Nostr.

You can also keep it just for yourself. Queued or published highlights cannot be deleted here.
