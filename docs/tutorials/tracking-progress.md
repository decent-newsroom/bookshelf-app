# Track a book

Status: Approved by the user and integrated into the in-app catalog, 2026-10-09. Part of the [tutorials and authoring guide](../tutorials.md).

Topic ID: `tracking_progress`.

Summary: Keep your current reads together and enjoy seeing how far you have come.

## 1. Put your next read in the spotlight

Step ID: `start_tracking`

Want this book waiting for you on Home? With it open, show the reader menus. Check whether new tracking is On this device or Public on Nostr, then tap Track progress beside the position information.

The book appears in Reading now on Home. If section order is unavailable, tracking is not ready yet.

## 2. Look back without losing your place

Step ID: `understand_progress`

Feel like rereading a favourite bit? Your current position can move backward while tracking remembers the furthest section you have reached. Automatic resume keeps the spot where you stopped on this device.

For sharing choices, open Settings > Reading progress & privacy. Public updates may show pending delivery; use Sync when it is available to retry synchronization.

## 3. A fresh start—or a finished book

Step ID: `manage_tracking`

Starting again? Use Reset tracking. Putting this book aside? Use Stop tracking to remove it from your reading list. Both keep your saved reading position and finished history.

Reached the end? Take a moment to enjoy it, then use Finish to record completion. Finishing is your choice; reaching the final chapter alone does not mark the book finished. Your finished books appear at the bottom of Home, newest first.
